Dentry — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Dentry is an app for recording the condition of a rental car at pick-up and return with photos and producing PDF reports. It is designed so that your data stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run any server, we have no user accounts, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Checks, photos and the details you type (car, plate, rental company, names, phone, email) are stored only on your device.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo gallery are used only when you choose to add a photo. Photos are resized and saved inside the app's private storage on your device, with their metadata removed (including any GPS location). We never upload them.

Sharing

A report leaves your phone only when you choose to send it (for example by email or a messaging app). That transfer is handled by the app you choose, under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play, which handles the payment under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details; Google Play tells the app only whether Pro has been purchased on your account.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

Deleting your data

Delete a check inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

Dentry is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: s.vistoryapp@gmail.com

Política de privacidade (Português)

Não recolhemos dados. A Dentry não tem servidores, contas, publicidade nem estatísticas de utilização. Vistorias, fotos e dados introduzidos ficam apenas no seu telemóvel. A câmara e a galeria só são usadas quando adiciona uma foto. Um relatório só sai do telemóvel quando decide enviá-lo. A compra Pro é processada pelo Google Play; não recebemos dados de pagamento. Para apagar os dados, apague a vistoria na app ou desinstale-a. Contacto: s.vistoryapp@gmail.com
